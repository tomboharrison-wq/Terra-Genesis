TERRA GENESIS FOR MAC — HOW TO INSTALL
=======================================

1. Double-click the zip you downloaded (if your Mac has not already opened it).
   You will see "Terra Genesis" (with a mountain icon).

2. Drag "Terra Genesis" into your Applications folder.

3. The first time only: macOS will not open apps from outside the App Store
   that have not been checked by Apple, so do this once:

   a) Open Applications, RIGHT-click (or Control-click) "Terra Genesis"
      and choose "Open".
   b) A warning appears. Click "Open" (or "Open Anyway").

   If there is no "Open" button (newer versions of macOS):
   a) Double-click the app once and click "Done" or "OK" on the warning.
   b) Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the
      message about "Terra Genesis" and click "Open Anyway".
   c) Enter your Mac password, then click "Open Anyway" again.

   If macOS says the app "is damaged and can't be opened":
   this is macOS being cautious about a downloaded app, not real damage.
   Open Terminal (Applications → Utilities → Terminal), paste this line
   and press Return:

       xattr -cr "/Applications/Terra Genesis.app"

   then open the app again.

4. After the first time, open it like any other app (Launchpad, Dock or
   Spotlight: press Cmd+Space and type "Terra").

WHAT YOU GET
- The whole of Terra Genesis in its own window, working fully offline.
- Saved worlds are kept on this Mac (Save / Load). Export files go to the
  folder you choose (usually Downloads).
- Landforms (top bar): every GCSE landform, one click to play.
- Lessons, Challenges, Checkpoints, Hazards, Meteor, Time-lapse, Export.
- Full screen for the projector: View → Enter Full Screen (Ctrl+Cmd+F),
  or the "Present" button in Landforms.

Which download?
- Apple Silicon Macs (M1, M2, M3, M4 — most Macs from 2021 on):
  Terra-Genesis-mac-apple-silicon.zip
- Older Intel Macs: Terra-Genesis-mac-intel.zip
  (Apple menu → About This Mac shows "Chip: Apple M…" or "Processor: Intel")

The online version is always at https://terra-genesis.netlify.app
